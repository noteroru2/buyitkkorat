# 06 — Internal Linking

## Hub-and-spoke

```
Home → Service hub → Product money pages
Home → City hub → Amphoe spokes
Articles → Matching money pages + City hub
FAQ → Service / City / About / Contact
Area layout → City hub / About / Contact
```

| Source | Target | Anchor theme | Placement |
|--------|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Thin articles | Money pages + city hub | Contextual Thai | Body |

No link farm footers; no exact-match spam. Orphans remain 0.
