# 08 — AEO, GEO, and E-E-A-T

## AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

### Strengths
- FAQ blocks + `FAQPage` schema on key templates  
- How-to articles (wipe data, photos, shipping, company docs)  
- Clear price disclaimer in `SITE.priceDisclaimer`  
- Step-oriented service copy on hubs  

### Weaknesses
- Many article answers are short (&lt;400 words) → weak snippet depth  
- FAQ hub `/คำถามที่พบบ่อย` ~219 words — too thin as answer hub  
- Limited comparison tables / dated price ranges on this domain  
- Author is Organization only — no named reviewer  

### Page ratings (sample)

| `/` | Strong | FAQ + clear process |
| `/วิธีประเมินราคา` | Strong/Medium | Intent-matched |

| Thin articles | Weak | Insufficient extractable depth |
| `/คำถามที่พบบ่อย` | Weak | Thin hub |
| Amphoe thin | Weak | Little unique Q&A |

**AEO score: 72**

## GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

### Strengths
- Consistent brand tokens: WINNER IT, legal name, LINE, phone  
- Honest negative claims (what they don’t claim) — useful for AI trust  
- robots Allow-all  
- Service ontology clear (buyback categories)  

### Weaknesses
- Physical HQ entity incomplete vs brief  
- Little first-party data (case studies, dated ranges, photos of real ops)  
- No Maps/GBP corroboration on-page  
- Risk of AI citing sister site vs this domain inconsistently
